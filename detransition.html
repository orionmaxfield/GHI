This page is separate on purpose.

The challenges before a transition and the challenges after one are not the same — medically, socially, or emotionally. Folding your situation into everyone else’s felt dishonest, so we didn’t.

What you can expect here

No agenda, and no vindication. You will not be treated as evidence for anyone’s argument — not ours, not anybody’s. You’re a person navigating something genuinely hard, often with less support than almost anyone else in this conversation.

What we offer is the same careful matching we offer everyone: an independent licensed therapist, found for your specific situation, who understands that detransition carries its own griefs, its own logistics, and its own questions about identity and relationships — and who will meet you there without judgment.

Getting started

Email us whatever feels sayable. You don’t need to explain your whole history in a first message — a few sentences is enough for us to start, and our intake coordinator will follow up for a free fifteen-minute conversation.

You are heard here, you are valued here, and there is support for you here.

Email us

If you or someone you love is in immediate crisis, please don't wait for an email reply. In the US, call or text 988, or contact your local emergency number.
