Email us

Reach out. We’ll take it from there.

Send us a note below, or email info@genderharmony.institute directly. There’s no wrong way to begin — “I don’t know where to start” is a perfectly good first line.

Send us a message

By sending, you agree to our Privacy Policy. Please don’t include medical records or detailed health history.

What happens next

We’ll reply by email to set up a free fifteen-minute conversation with our intake coordinator — a chance to talk through your situation and what options exist, before any commitments.

A note on urgency

We answer messages as fast as one small organization can, but we are not a crisis line. If you or someone you love is in immediate danger, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number, right now.

If you or someone you love is in immediate crisis, please don't wait for an email reply. In the US, call or text 988, or contact your local emergency number.
