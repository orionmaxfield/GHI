Email us

One email starts it.

Write to info@genderharmony.institute in your own words. There’s no form to get right and no wrong way to begin — “I don’t know where to start” is a perfectly good first line.

Email info@genderharmony.institute

If it helps, include:

  • Who the support is for (yourself, your child, a friend) and roughly their age
  • Your state — therapy licensing works state by state
  • Anything about your values or situation you want a therapist to understand
  • How urgent things feel right now

We’ll reply to set up a free fifteen-minute conversation with our intake coordinator — a chance to talk through your situation and what options exist, before any commitments.

If you’d rather talk than write, say so in your email and we’ll schedule a call.

A note on urgency

We answer email as fast as one small organization can, but we are not a crisis line. If you or someone you love is in immediate danger, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number, right now.

If you or someone you love is in immediate crisis, please don't wait for an email reply. In the US, call or text 988, or contact your local emergency number.
