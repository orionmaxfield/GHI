Your pace. Your path.

Wherever you are with questions of gender and identity, you deserve a therapist who will actually listen — not sort you into a box, not push you toward any particular outcome, and not treat your values or your family as obstacles.

What working with us looks like

  1. Reach out Email us in your own words. If you’re under 18, we’ll speak with a parent or guardian first.
  2. A real conversation Our intake coordinator talks with you (free, about fifteen minutes) about what you’re looking for and what matters to you in a therapist.
  3. A careful match We look for an independent licensed clinician suited to you — your situation, your values, your state. Telehealth means geography isn’t the limit it used to be.

What we believe about your care

Good therapy here starts with clarity — space to articulate who you are and what you value, without anyone else’s script. From that ground, it becomes possible to invest in relationships with people in your life who may believe differently, without losing yourself in the process.

That’s the whole idea: growing whole in who you are, in your relationships, and in what you’re capable of.
