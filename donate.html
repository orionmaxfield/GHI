Keep the middle ground open.

We’re a small nonprofit, and the quiet parts of this work — the free intake conversations, the hours of searching for the right therapist, the resources we give away — are what your gift pays for.

What a gift funds

Free intake for every family. No one pays to talk to us, ever. Donations keep it that way.

Careful matching. Finding a therapist for this work takes real hours per family. Gifts cover the coordination that no one is billed for.

Resources anyone can use. Guides and materials for parents, individuals, and advocates — free, for families who may never become clients.
